Section six secretly takes rights to train artificial intelligence models on your company data. Outside counsel costs eight hundred dollars an hour and takes three weeks. Founders cannot wait, so they sign blind. We built Redline to give every operator an attorney-grade contract safety instrument that audits incoming terms against open standards in seconds.

Corporate contracts contain sensitive pricing, intellectual property disclosures, and confidential trade secrets. They should never be uploaded to remote cloud servers or used to train third-party models. Redline is architected with one hundred percent client-side execution. Our parser extracts structured OpenXML trees directly in browser memory using Web Workers. Zero documents leave your machine, zero bytes are logged to a server, and zero telemetry is collected.

Redline ships pre-loaded with three common predatory templates. First: an aggressive SaaS agreement with unlimited customer exposure and twenty percent annual price hikes. Second: a mutual NDA with a disguised two-year non-compete backdoor and perpetual confidentiality. Third: a consulting agreement forcing ninety-day delayed payouts and pre-payment intellectual property forfeiture. Operators see the plain English business stakes first, while counsel can inspect exact clause numbers and statutory risk tiers.

Instead of relying on unpredictable language model prompts that hallucinate and vary on every run, Redline benchmarks contracts deterministically against open-source Common Paper standards. Common Paper agreements are MIT licensed, drafted and vetted by a committee of leading commercial attorneys. Our engine executes in under twenty milliseconds, comparing vendor wording against battle-tested market consensus.

Instead of tedious negotiations, operators can remediate the entire contract in one click. Clicking Accept All Common Paper Fixes runs our Myers diff engine in memory, replacing every predatory clause with balanced standard language. The scorecard instantly transforms from Grade F to Grade A Redlined, with full reversibility if you want to keep specific vendor terms.

Redline synthesizes genuine Office OpenXML insertion and deletion nodes into a native Microsoft Word dot docx file. When opposing counsel opens it in Word or Google Docs, it renders authentic tracked revisions. Alongside the document, you get an executive pushback email ready to paste and send.
